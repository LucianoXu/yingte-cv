\section{Research Summary}

My research applies formal methods to study programming theory for quantum computing, including semantics, verification techniques, and proof automation. The aim is a practical toolchain that can naturally encode and verify quantum algorithms and protocols. Recent work also explores machine learning and LLM-based coding agents as a means toward this automation.
